I'm Sujith. I grew up in Malmö, moved to Hyderabad for high school, chose to spend a term in Kenya to learn about the people and cultures there, and then came to Warwick to study economics, psychology and philosophy together. The longer version is in [my journey](/journey/malmo).

Right now I'm looking for my first role in consulting or at a scale-up, somewhere I can build real business sense and the confidence to run projects on my own.

While I look, I'm working through Aswath Damodaran's valuation course, learning Swedish and building AI tools. I also make time for the gym, friends and journalling. I'm happy with where I am, and having clear goals to work towards makes the effort fulfilling.

When nobody's asking, I read about scientific discoveries, economics playing out in the real world, new AI tools and what people do with them, and the stories of founders, to see what I can learn. I also spend time on Hinduism, which to me is a way of life, and it shapes how I think about living by my values and giving back to the people around me.

Over the next few years I want to get good at leading bigger pieces of work with other people, and at spotting where something could run better or where an opportunity is waiting.
