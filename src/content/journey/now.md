I'm based in London now. My normal days are about upskilling myself: working through Aswath Damodaran's valuation course and the McKinsey Forward Programme, learning Swedish and building AI tools, with time for the gym, friends and journalling. I'm happy with where I am, and having clear goals to work towards makes the effort fulfilling.

One idea I really liked from Damodaran is that the technical side of valuation is relatively straightforward once you take note of it. The art lies in how you choose to view each variable and what values you choose to use. A company that operates in several countries may need a country risk premium, for example, and you could base it on where the company is or on where its revenue comes from. From the McKinsey programme I took a problem breakdown framework. I used to think about the solution space, its constraints and stakeholder requirements after stating a problem. Now they are a step inside the problem statement, and that really challenges me to find the main point.

I'm also curious about how else I can use AI right now. I'm building an AI valuation bot, based on what I've learned, that will efficiently collect the information I need to value a company, as well as an investment agent tailored to my needs.

Bringing change that matters, to me, means putting myself in situations where I can build business acumen and the confidence to lead projects by myself. Then I can use my skills to identify pressing issues for the people or businesses around me, and help them develop and become more efficient. My latest example is a 180 Degrees Consulting project last year, where my team built an AI strategy for ReAll that was named among the top 2% of 180DC projects worldwide. To build that acumen and confidence, I want to join a scale-up or an SME where I can tackle different tasks, or work as a business analyst, business growth analyst or strategy consultant.
